## 🧑‍💻 About Me

<img align="right" width="28%" src="https://github.com/7oSkaaa/7oSkaaa/blob/main/Images/Right_Side.gif" />

Hey! I'm **Abdulla Rahaman**, also known as **CatHaxor**.

I'm a developer and technology enthusiast from Bangladesh. I enjoy exploring how technology works, building practical software, solving technical problems, and experimenting with ideas that turn into real-world projects.

My interests span software development, cybersecurity, automation, Linux infrastructure, and artificial intelligence.

I don't believe learning should stop at tutorials. I prefer building things, experimenting, debugging, understanding the underlying concepts, and continuously improving.

### A little more about me

- 🔐 Learning and exploring cybersecurity and ethical hacking.
- 💻 Developing software, utilities, APIs and backend systems.
- 🤖 Building automation tools, bots and integrations.
- 🐧 Exploring Linux, system administration and self-hosting.
- ☁️ Working with VPS environments, Docker and deployment.
- 🧠 Experimenting with AI, LLMs and locally hosted models.
- 🎨 Interested in modern UI/UX and desktop application development.
- 🌱 Always curious about new technologies and engineering ideas.

> "I don't just want to use technology. I want to understand it, build with it, and make something meaningful."
